### BR-007 — Half-Day Ratio Hardcoded (0.5)
**Original severity:** LOW
**Layers affected:** DB · API

0.5 is a business constant (1 half-day = 0.5 effective days). Making it configurable would add complexity with no v1 requirement. Decision: retain hardcoded, document as constant.

Canonical formula note in 08.6 DB §12 explicitly states `halfDays × 0.5` as the business rule.

**Status: RESOLVED (by design — documented constant)**
